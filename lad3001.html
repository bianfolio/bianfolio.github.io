SELECTED WORK · Y3S1 · 2026

Where We Retreat,
Forests Return

How can we reconnect a heritage landscape with campus life while leaving room for the forest to change?

Site
Kent Ridge, Singapore
Course
NUS · LAD3001 Design 5
Author
Bian Chuqi
Work
Academic design proposal

DESIGN POSITION

A lighter
footprint.

Retreat becomes a deliberate spatial choice.

The proposal approaches Kent Ridge as a living military heritage landscape. Historical memory, forest succession and campus life shape where people approach, pause and step back.

Lightweight trails adapt to changing conditions. Bungalows become places for interpretation and shared activities; the outpost offers close encounters with vegetation and material traces.

Reading before connecting

From historical and ecological research to selective access.

History

Ridge-scale research traced changing land uses and forms of remembrance across NUS1 and KRP1.

Ecology

Vegetation transects and an individual section of the ruin examined canopy, understory, material surfaces and microhabitats.

Response

Eight opportunity nodes form a spatial framework. Terrain, roots, vegetation and operational access guide which connections can proceed.

STRUCTURE & ADAPTATION

Walking
lightly.

Repeatable deck modules, turns, stairs and widened pauses allow individual trail sections to be adjusted or removed. Open-grid decking lets light, rain and litter pass through.

Where conditions are sensitive, the response can be to reroute rather than continually raise the deck.

Dimensions and details are design intentions, subject to structural and site verification.

Coexistence and retreat

Necessary access and ecological retirement are different states.

STUDIO PRESENTATION

Research into
a shared story.

Transects, sections, diagrams and perspectives bring the historical and ecological readings into one design narrative.

Project credits

Research contribution: Graphic design and visual consolidation for Projects 1 and 2; individual field observations and section studies. Historical research, interpretation and selected transect elements were collaborative.

Digital production: Illustrator assembly and editing; MidJourney-assisted graphic exploration and model-based imagery; ChatGPT-assisted text organisation and critique, as acknowledged in the source portfolio.
